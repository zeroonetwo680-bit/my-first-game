Privacy Policy

NEON WRECKAGE — Android application com.neonwreckage.game
Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version: NEON WRECKAGE collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no network permission. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask for an account, an email address, a phone number, a date of birth, a location or any other personal information.

2. What is stored on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data in your browser/app local storage so it can remember your progress between sessions:

  • your high scores and maximum combo,
  • which achievements you have unlocked,
  • your sound and vibration preferences,
  • a flag recording that you have already seen the tutorial prompts.

This data never leaves the device. It is not transmitted to us, to Google, or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage in Android settings, deletes all of it permanently.

3. Permissions

The application does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or any other sensitive permission. Its Android manifest contains no network permission, which means the app is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

4. Third parties

There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporters and no social logins in this application. Nothing is shared with third parties, because nothing is collected in the first place.

5. Children

The game contains stylised arcade action with no blood, no gore and no realistic violence. It is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR provisions on children's data by virtue of not processing any such data.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar regulations you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase; the only data that exists is on your own device and you can delete it at any time from Android's App info screen.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever adds a feature that collects data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the game in general, can be sent to the developer through the contact details on the NEON WRECKAGE Google Play listing, or by opening an issue at github.com/zeroonetwo680-bit/my-first-game/issues.

NEON WRECKAGE is an independent game. This policy applies to the Android application distributed on Google Play and to the web build.
